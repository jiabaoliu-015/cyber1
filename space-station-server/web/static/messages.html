Welcome to Erebus

Operated by Tallis Group, Erebus is home to five dedicated crew members and ORACLE, the station AI that would like to remind you it is not a crew member and does not cover extra shifts.

Our mission is to support scientific research, maintain essential systems, and demonstrate that five people can share a workplace without opening an airlock over a minor disagreement.

Tallis Group: bringing humanity closer to the stars, and slightly further from technical support.
