Research Note — Object K-9 Recovery — Station Erebus
Date: 2026-08-27 | Authors: EVA team (Becker, Mercer) + Researcher Wu Yan

During hull survey at frame 88 aft, recovered a basalt-like mass ~40cm,
pitted, faintly warm to the glove despite -40C ambient. No transponder,
no alloy match. Brought inside per science protocol, cradled in aft hold
(service shaft D-4 adjacent, near HVAC return trunk — in hindsight, poor placement).

Initial readings: 12C in airlock, 19C after 6h, 28C after 24h, now 38C and
still climbing slowly (see object_thermal_output.txt). No power source found.
Condensation beads on nearby ducts in patterns crew keep photographing —
Becker has filled a notebook with them.

Handling: gloves + buddy system. Do NOT sleep near it. Do NOT lick it
(yes, this had to be written down after the condensate incident).
Research continues; HVAC impact is now the priority.

No flag in the rock. It is hot, strange, and flagless.
