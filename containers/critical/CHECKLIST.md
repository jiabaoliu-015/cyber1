# Critical owner checklist — offline artifacts and proposed services

**Scope:** `containers/critical/` only. This checklist owns the local/offline reverse-engineering and advanced artifacts, their deterministic generation/reset/verification, and truthful handoff evidence. It does not claim a five-container runtime, a working reactor or black-box service, or completion-checker ownership. Keep required challenges independently solvable; the separate completion checker aggregates evidence and is not a challenge unlock mechanism.

## Current inventory and status

Critical currently **houses** `RE-01` Runtime Secret and `RE-02` Validator Inversion alongside `ADV-01` Crypto Misuse and `ADV-02` Oracle Analysis. The final plot assigns only **one** RE to Critical; the team must choose which of RE-01/02 moves elsewhere. `RE-03` Real-vs-Decoy is assigned to the standalone Medbay Terminal in Operations; the legacy Critical build/test harness compiles its canonical source there for compatibility. `OPT-01` remains optional and excluded from required coverage.

The two proposed narrative destinations are **not implemented services**: `services/reactor-control/README.md` says reserved; `services/black-box/README.md` says reserved and distinguishes the existing offline artifacts from a standalone black-box service. Do not present either as working, add invented endpoints/flags, or imply that five layout directories equal five running containers. Implement either only under a separately reviewed, coordinated scope with a defined owner, learning objective, independent route, acceptance test, safe runtime boundary, reset, and integration contract.
