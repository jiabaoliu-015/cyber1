Correlation is now undeniable:

  Object surface: 12C (08-27) -> 28C (08-28) -> 34C (08-31) -> 38.2C (09-02)

  Station return:    23.8C -> 24.1C -> 27.6C -> 28.3C

The object leads, the station follows by ~12-18h. Every degree the cradle gains,
the HVAC return gains ~0.6 degrees half a day later. The plant was sized for
people and machines, not a slow 38C heater plumbed into the return trunk.

Effect on crew: heat rash, insomnia, headaches (see sickbay_heat_cases.txt),
plus the low hum (see acoustic_hum_analysis.txt). Becker is the most affected
because he spends the most time near it. Distance helps — Deck A sleeps better
than Deck D. Nobody is possessed; everybody is overheated and underslept.
