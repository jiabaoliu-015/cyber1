KNOWN ISSUES - status link            (personal log, not for distribution)

right. i'm keeping a list of the truly inspired engineering on this station, so
that one day i can track down the visionaries responsible and shake their hands.
i would genuinely love to meet the sleep-deprived monkey who designed this
network, because there is no way a sober human signed off on it.

exhibit A, the status link, my personal favourite:

  - ground control believes anything the relay hands it and never once checks
    whether it's current. i watched it report "nominal" off a frame from last
    week. it cannot tell old from new. you could feed it a postcard.

  - ORACLE at least asks for a signature, a low bar, gloriously unmet, because
    the signing key is still the factory default sitting right next to the relay
    config. i filed the rotation ticket. twice. "the AI keeps us safe." sure.

  - and nothing on the segment is encrypted. nothing. the whole thing's in the
    clear. you wouldn't have to break in, just stand in the middle and listen.

i started this list as a joke. i'm not laughing anymore. something here is wrong,
and if the link ever goes quiet and stays quiet, it was me, or whoever read this
and did the sensible thing. rebooting won't help; it just puts it all back the
way they like it.

if you need to know how it's actually wired, it's written in the maintenance
note, courtesy of our resident engineer, aka the grumpiest man in space.
