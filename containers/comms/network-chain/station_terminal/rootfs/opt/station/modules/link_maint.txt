right. i am writing this ONCE so the next person who comms me at 0300 about
"ground's dropped again" can read it themselves and leave me alone.

how it actually works, since nobody reads the manual: the relay (comms_relay)
does the talking. every few seconds it wraps a status frame and fires it at TWO
places:
  - down to ground through the ground_link receiver (landfall). yes, that's the
    one command stares at.
  - across to the sentry, so the AI stops nagging that we're dead.
the numbers in the frame come from the event log. it's not magic.

when ground "looks stuck" (it's ALWAYS this):
  1. reboot the relay. that's the fix 9 times out of 10. it re-reads its config
     on the way up.
  2. still showing an old status? that's ground_link being lazy. it only keeps
     the "last status seen" and won't budge until it accepts a new frame. not my
     system, not my problem.
  3. sentry throwing a fit? that's the fussy one, picky about what it accepts.
     go poke the relay's signing config, not me.

and before someone "helpfully" packet-dumps the whole segment again: yes it's
all in the clear in here, that's normal for a closed net, no it is not a crisis,
put wireshark down.
