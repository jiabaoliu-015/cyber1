Support Centre

Forward Logistics Command Technical Services provides assistance for approved personnel experiencing operational workstation issues.

Common Support Topics
  • Printer connectivity for logistics manifests.
  • Personnel workstation redeployment after terminal changes.
  • Authentication and account lockout requests.
  • Network availability for forward operating stations.

Service Desk Guidance

Operational profiles are saved automatically while personnel remain signed in.

If your profile does not appear after signing in from a different secure workstation, begin Personnel Redeployment to restore your most recent local checkpoint.

For communications outages affecting operational reporting, contact the Regional Communications Officer through the secure service desk.

Maintenance Window

Routine maintenance is scheduled every Sunday between 0200–0300 UTC. During this period, archived logistics records may remain available.
